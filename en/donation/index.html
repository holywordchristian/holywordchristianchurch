INTERAC E-TRANSFER · GIVING

Give by Interac e-Transfer

Thank you for supporting the church through prayer and giving. Please confirm your details so we can record your gift correctly.

How to give

  1. Log in to your online banking.
  2. Select Holy Word Christian Church in your Interac e-Transfer recipients, or send to holywordchristian@gmail.com.
  3. Include your name, address, email, and the purpose of your gift in the message box.
  4. No security question is required. Complete the transfer.
Note: Returning givers can simply include their name, email, and giving purpose.
